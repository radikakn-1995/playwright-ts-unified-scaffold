# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Employees receive 18 days of annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 1.5 days per month.
Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year.
Carried-forward days expire on 31 March of the following year.
Annual leave of more than 3 days must be applied for at least 7 days in advance.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per year.
A medical certificate is required for more than 2 consecutive days of sick leave.

## Parental leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks.
Paternity leave is 10 working days, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.

## How to apply
All leave is applied for in OrangeHRM under Leave > Apply.
